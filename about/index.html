Born From an Obsession

Anthony DiGiovanni & Tanner Dilworth

The studio collectors and racers call when a car has to be exactly right.

Anthony DiGiovanni

Founder

Carrara Design wasn’t born from a business plan. It was born from an obsession. In 2010, at 12 years old, Anthony started digitally “unmasking” Porsche prototypes, a habit sparked by the sight of a Fayence Yellow Carrera GT.

The work was soon picked up by Top Gear, Autoblog and Jalopnik. By 2015 his livery designs were winning Best of Show, and by 2017 Carrara Design was a full-time studio.

Tanner Dilworth

Creative Director

Tanner’s obsession started by accident. He realized a 986 Boxster was within reach and bought one with money from his summer landscaping business.

A cinematic film he made of a Carrara Design livery caught Anthony’s attention. Today he brings a fresh eye and his own aesthetic to the studio’s most exclusive builds.

Our story

From Render to Reality

Real cars have been part of the work from the very beginning. Project 001, a Maritime Blue Paint to Sample 911 GT3 RS 4.0, came in 2010, the same year it all started, and set the tone for projects that bridge the gap between enthusiast dreams and factory reality.

By 2017, Carrara Design had become a full-time studio, consulting on bespoke specifications for Pagani, Koenigsegg and Porsche’s elite Sonderwunsch program.

We quickly became a trusted partner for collectors worldwide, eventually assisting with the first-ever factory Porsche Carrera GT recommission in Oak Green Metallic. A full-circle moment, back to the yellow car that started it all.

Our deep dive into the enthusiast world, combined with a passion for driving, connected us with rising racing talent. That eventually led to the opportunity to design championship-winning liveries for drivers and teams at the top levels of the sport, in series like Porsche’s Carrera Cup and IMSA’s WeatherTech SportsCar Championship.

Our craft

Made by Hand

Every commission starts from a blank file. We model each car from scratch in 3D, then build up the paint, leather, stitching and wheels by hand, until the render matches what the factory will actually build.

Not a single pixel is generated by AI. We have nothing against it for the mundane, but creating art is a human job, whether it’s a render on screen or the real car it becomes.

We take protecting that craft seriously. It’s also what makes creative direction worth having: every decision is made by people who care how the car turns out, which is how we help our clients get the most from their creative investment.

What’s next

Zennsport is our performance coachbuilding division, and the next chapter of the same story: from specifying the car to building it. The name joins Rennsport, the racing heritage behind Porsche’s RS cars, with Zen, a calm presence behind the wheel.

Its first concept, the R Spyder, reimagines the 986 Boxster as the mid-engine GT Porsche never built. Real GT pace, road comfort and a Sonderwunsch-style interior, starting with Tanner’s own 2000 Boxster S as Chassis 001.

The R Spyder is only the beginning. Our goal is to bring the Zennsport philosophy to more chassis, and eventually to more marques.
