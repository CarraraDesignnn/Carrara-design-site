Tanner Dilworth & Anthony DiGiovanni

The studio collectors and racers call when a car has to be exactly right.

Anthony DiGiovanni

Founder

Anthony DiGiovanni was twelve when he began digitally “unmasking” Porsche prototypes, imagining each car before the factory revealed it. The spark was a Fayence Yellow Carrera GT he saw as a kid.

Those renders found their way to Top Gear, Autoblog and Jalopnik, and by 2015 his livery designs were winning Best of Show. What began as a fascination became Carrara Design.

Tanner Dilworth

Creative Director

Tanner Dilworth found Porsche almost by accident, when he realized a 986 Boxster was within reach and bought one with money from his summer landscaping business.

The car became a passion, and a film he made of a Carrara Design livery caught Anthony’s attention. Today he brings that same eye to the studio’s most exclusive client builds.

Milestones

Along the Way

Our story

From Render to Reality

Our first full specification was Project 001, a Maritime Blue Paint to Sample 911 GT3 RS 4.0, delivered in 2012. Since then we have helped collectors around the world see their cars before they are built, and refine every choice until it feels right. Many of the cars we design go on to become some of the most renowned examples of their chassis. That work has given us a close understanding of the most bespoke programs in the world, from Porsche Sonderwunsch, Pagani and Koenigsegg to the restomod builders who take a classic further than the factory ever did.

We quickly became a trusted partner for collectors worldwide, eventually assisting with the first-ever factory Porsche Carrera GT recommission in Oak Green Metallic: a full-circle moment from the yellow car that started it all.

Our deep dive into the enthusiast world, combined with a passion for driving, connected us with rising racing talent. That eventually led to championship-winning liveries for drivers and teams at the top levels of the sport, in series like Porsche’s Carrera Cup and IMSA’s WeatherTech SportsCar Championship.

Our craft

Made by Hand

Every commission starts from a blank file. We model each car from scratch in 3D, then build up the paint, leather, stitching and wheels by hand, until the render matches what the factory will actually build.

Not a single pixel is generated by AI. We have nothing against it for the mundane, but creating art is a human job, whether it’s a render on screen or the real car it becomes.

We take protecting that craft seriously. It’s also what makes creative direction worth having: every decision is made by people who care how the car turns out, which is how we help our clients get the most from their creative investment.

What’s next

After years of specifying cars for clients, we started building our own. Zennsport is our performance coachbuilding division. The name joins Rennsport, the racing heritage behind Porsche’s RS cars, with Zen, for a calm presence behind the wheel.

Its first concept, the R Spyder, reimagines the 986 Boxster as the mid-engine GT Porsche never built. Real GT pace, road comfort and a Sonderwunsch-style interior, starting with Tanner’s own 2000 Boxster S as Chassis 001.

The R Spyder is only the beginning. Our goal is to bring the Zennsport philosophy to more chassis, and eventually to more marques.
