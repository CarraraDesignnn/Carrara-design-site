Anthony DiGiovanni

Founder

Carrara Design wasn’t born from a business plan, it was born from an obsession. The journey began in 2010, when 12-year-old Anthony DiGiovanni started digitally “unmasking” Porsche prototypes.

What began as a childhood spark, ignited by the sighting of a Fayence Yellow Carrera GT, quickly turned into international attention from publications like Top Gear, Autoblog and Jalopnik. By 2015, a hobby became a business, as Anthony’s livery designs began winning Best of Show awards on the global stage.

Tanner Dilworth

Creative Director

Today, the studio welcomes a new generation of talent sharing the same passion. Tanner Dilworth’s journey began with an accidental Porsche obsession: realizing a 986 Boxster was within reach, he bought one with money from his summer landscaping business.

A deep passion for the brand followed, and led him to film a cinematic video of a Carrara Design livery that caught Anthony’s attention. Tanner now brings a fresh taste and perspective to the team, applying his creative skill and aesthetic eye to help elevate the most exclusive client builds.

Our story

From Render to Reality

By 2017, Carrara Design had become a full-time studio, consulting on bespoke specifications for Pagani, Koenigsegg and Porsche’s elite Sonderwunsch program.

That work is defined by landmark projects that bridge the gap between enthusiast dreams and factory reality, a path that began with Project 001: a Maritime Blue Paint to Sample 911 GT3 RS 4.0.

We quickly became a trusted partner for collectors worldwide, eventually assisting with the first-ever factory Porsche Carrera GT recommission in Oak Green Metallic: a full-circle moment from the yellow car that started it all.

Our deep dive into the enthusiast world, combined with a passion for driving, connected us with rising racing talent. That eventually led to championship-winning liveries for drivers and teams at the top levels of the sport, in series like Porsche’s Carrera Cup and IMSA’s WeatherTech SportsCar Championship.

Our craft

Made by Hand

Every commission starts from a blank file. We model each car from scratch in 3D, then build up the paint, leather, stitching and wheels by hand, until the render matches what the factory will actually build.

Not a single pixel is generated by AI. We have nothing against it for the mundane, but creating art is a human job, whether it’s a render on screen or the real car it becomes.

We take protecting that craft seriously. It’s also what makes creative direction worth having: every decision is made by people who care how the car turns out, which is how we help our clients get the most from their creative investment.

What’s next

Zennsport is our performance coachbuilding division, and the next chapter of the same story: from specifying the car to building it. The name joins Rennsport, the racing heritage behind Porsche’s RS cars, with Zen, a calm presence behind the wheel.

Its first concept, the R Spyder, reimagines the 986 Boxster as the mid-engine GT Porsche never built. Real GT pace, road comfort and a Sonderwunsch-style interior, starting with Tanner’s own 2000 Boxster S as Chassis 001.

The R Spyder is only the beginning. Our goal is to bring the Zennsport philosophy to more chassis, and eventually to more marques.
