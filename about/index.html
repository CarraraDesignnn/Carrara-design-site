Born From an Obsession

Anthony DiGiovanni & Tanner Dilworth

The studio collectors and racers call when a car has to be exactly right.

Anthony DiGiovanni

Founder

Carrara Design wasn’t born from a business plan. It was born from an obsession. In 2010, at 12 years old, Anthony started digitally “unmasking” Porsche prototypes, a habit sparked by the sight of a Fayence Yellow Carrera GT.

The work was soon picked up by Top Gear, Autoblog and Jalopnik. By 2015 his livery designs were winning Best of Show, and by 2017 Carrara Design was a full-time studio.

Tanner Dilworth

Creative Director

Tanner’s obsession started by accident. He realized a 986 Boxster was within reach and bought one with money from his summer landscaping business.

A cinematic film he made of a Carrara Design livery caught Anthony’s attention. Today he brings a fresh eye and his own aesthetic to the studio’s most exclusive builds.

From Render to Reality

Made by Hand

Every commission is modeled from scratch in 3D and directed by people, never generated. Technology has its place in the mundane, but never in the art itself, on screen or on the road.

We believe human craft is worth protecting, and that real creative direction is what makes a commission worth the investment.

What’s next

Zennsport is our performance coachbuilding division, and the next chapter of the same story: from specifying the car to building it. The name joins Rennsport, the racing heritage behind Porsche’s RS cars, with Zen, a calm presence behind the wheel.

Its first concept, the R Spyder, reimagines the 986 Boxster as the mid-engine GT Porsche never built. Real GT pace, road comfort and a Sonderwunsch-style interior, starting with Tanner’s own 2000 Boxster S as Chassis 001.

The R Spyder is only the beginning. Our goal is to bring the Zennsport philosophy to more chassis, and eventually to more marques.
