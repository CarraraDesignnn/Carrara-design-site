2010

Born From an Obsession

Carrara Design wasn’t born from a business plan. It was born from an obsession. In 2010, a 12-year-old Anthony DiGiovanni started digitally “unmasking” Porsche prototypes, a habit sparked by the sight of a Fayence Yellow Carrera GT. Before long, the work was being picked up by Top Gear, Autoblog and Jalopnik.

By 2015 the hobby had become a business, with Anthony’s livery designs winning Best of Show on the global stage.

2017

Render to Reality

In 2017 Carrara Design became a full-time studio, and the work moved from digital renders to real cars. We began consulting on bespoke specifications for Pagani, Koenigsegg and Porsche’s Sonderwunsch program, starting with Project 001: a Maritime Blue Paint to Sample 911 GT3 RS 4.0.

Collectors around the world followed. One of them brought us the first Carrera GT ever recommissioned by the factory, finished in Oak Green Metallic. A full-circle moment, years after a yellow one started it all.

On track

Championship Liveries

Time in the enthusiast world and behind the wheel connected us with rising racing talent. That led to championship-winning liveries for drivers and teams in Porsche Carrera Cup and IMSA’s WeatherTech SportsCar Championship.

Today

A New Generation

Tanner Dilworth’s obsession started by accident. He realized a 986 Boxster was within reach and bought one with money from his summer landscaping business. A cinematic film he made of a Carrara Design livery caught Anthony’s attention.

Today Tanner brings a fresh eye and his own aesthetic to the studio, helping elevate our most exclusive client builds.

Our craft

Made by Hand

Every commission is modeled from scratch in 3D and directed by people. Software can handle the mundane, like the code behind this site, but never the art itself, on screen or on the road.

We believe human craft is worth protecting, and that real creative direction is what makes a commission worth the investment.

What’s next

Zennsport

In November 2026 we launched Zennsport, our performance coachbuilding division. Its first concept, the R Spyder, reimagines the 986 Boxster as the mid-engine GT Porsche never built. Chassis 001 is Tanner’s own 2000 Boxster S.

The same story, one step further: from specifying the car to building it.
