About

Factory configurators end where your imagination begins. Carrara Design is your personal design team — the studio collectors and racers call when a car has to be exactly right.

We visualize, ideate and refine every commission in photoreal renders, so you see the finished car — paint, leather, stitching and wheels — long before a single panel is sprayed.

From Porsche Sonderwunsch and Ferrari Tailor Made to full classic recommissions, we guide you through the factory bespoke process and translate your vision into a specification the builder can execute without compromise.
